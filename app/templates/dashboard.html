What does each scan check?

Light checks a smaller set of features across the detected local network. Deep checks many more features on one device and can take substantially longer.

In technical terms, Light checks 12 TCP and 3 UDP ports. Deep checks all 65,535 TCP ports and 25 selected UDP ports, with software identification and safe service checks.

Neither scan tries to break into devices or guess passwords. These checks cannot find every possible security problem or prove a device is safe.

Slow responses, larger networks and local AI preparation can add time. Progress is saved as checks finish, then the app prepares easier-to-understand explanations for your report.
