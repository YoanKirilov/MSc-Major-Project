Understanding your results

Does this mean someone broke in?

No such conclusion can be drawn from this scan. It checks how devices answer on your local network, not whether someone has used them without permission. No findings does not mean everything is safe.

What should I check first?

High means review first; Medium means review next; Low means lower priority. Review items describe features whose safety this tool has not assessed. These labels are not proof of a break-in.

What did the scan manage to check?

These results cover only the checks listed. If any checks could not finish, retrying them creates a new report and keeps this one.

Explain the words in this report
Service
A device feature that other devices can contact, such as file sharing or a web page. An open service is not automatically a security problem or an active connection.
IP address
A device's address on this network. It helps you match the result to your router's device list, but it can change.
Port
A numbered contact point for a device feature. TCP and UDP are two ways devices exchange information.
Encryption
Protection that scrambles information while it travels so it is not readable without the right key.
Confidence
How strongly the saved evidence supports a finding, not how safe the device is.
Unknown device
The scan did not obtain a usable name. This does not mean the device is an intruder. Check your router's device list or ask the person who set up the network.
