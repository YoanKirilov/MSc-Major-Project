Settings

Announcements are available for Light and Deep scans. Deep and known-host scans keep announcements only for the selected devices. Both profiles also collect bounded extra device details and compare recent saved reports where identity can be matched.

Plain-language results with Ollama

Every new scan includes local AI report preparation. The results open when that step finishes. If Ollama stops, you can retry the explanation using the saved scan.

Device names from Pi-hole (optional)

Pi-hole is a separate service on your own network. It can help recognise devices using names it already knows. Installing it alone does not identify every device.

For setup, follow docs/pihole-setup.md in the project. Keep this off on networks you are not authorised to assess. Imported names are extra context, not proof that a device is online or safe.
